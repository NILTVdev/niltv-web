Arbitration Agreement & Class Action Waiver

9.1 Agreement to Arbitrate

By participating in the NIL Star Singing Competition, each contestant agrees that any and all disputes, claims, or controversies arising out of or relating to the Competition, including but not limited to participation, eligibility, disqualification, media use, prizes, or interpretation of these terms, shall be resolved exclusively through final and binding arbitration. This agreement applies to all claims whether based in contract, tort, statute, regulation, or any other legal or equitable theory.

9.2 Governing Seat and Venue

The arbitration shall be conducted with the legal seat and venue in Monmouth County, New Jersey, United States. All hearings shall take place in Monmouth County, New Jersey.

9.3 Arbitration Administrator and Rules

Arbitration shall be administered by the American Arbitration Association, under its applicable commercial arbitration rules in effect at the time the dispute is filed. The arbitrator shall be selected in accordance with those rules and shall be a licensed attorney or retired judge with experience in commercial or entertainment law.

9.4 Waiver of Court Rights

All contestants acknowledge and agree that:

  • They are waiving the right to a trial by jury
  • They are waiving the right to bring claims in court except as specifically allowed below
  • They are waiving the right to participate in any class, collective, or representative action

9.5 Limited Court Exceptions

Notwithstanding the above, either party may bring an individual claim in a court of competent jurisdiction in Monmouth County, New Jersey solely for:

  • Injunctive or equitable relief to protect intellectual property or confidential information
  • Enforcement of the arbitration agreement
  • Confirmation or enforcement of an arbitration award

9.6 Discovery and Procedure

The parties agree that arbitration shall be conducted on an expedited and streamlined basis, including:

  • Limited discovery, restricted to narrowly tailored document exchange
  • No broad depositions unless expressly authorized by the arbitrator
  • Written submissions prioritized over live testimony where appropriate

The intent is to resolve disputes efficiently, fairly, and without unnecessary delay or expense.

9.7 Limitation of Arbitration Awards and Damages Cap

To the fullest extent permitted by applicable law, the contestant agrees that any claim, dispute, or cause of action arising out of or relating to the Competition, including any claim submitted to arbitration, shall be subject to the following limitation:

Maximum Recovery Cap. The total aggregate liability of NIL TV, Inc., its affiliates, sponsors, officers, employees, contractors, and agents for any and all claims, damages, awards, or relief of any kind arising out of or relating to the Competition shall not exceed, in any circumstance, the actual monetary value of the prize awarded to the contestant (if any).

No Additional Damages. In no event shall any contestant be entitled to recover, and the arbitrator shall have no authority to award, any indirect, incidental, consequential, special, exemplary, or punitive damages, or any damages for lost profits, lost opportunities, reputational harm, emotional distress, or similar claims.

Exclusive Remedy Limitation. The contestant acknowledges and agrees that the prize (if awarded) constitutes the full and exclusive maximum remedy available for any claim arising from participation in the Competition.

Acknowledgment of Bargained For Exchange. Contestant acknowledges that participation in the Competition, including eligibility for any prize, constitutes adequate consideration for this limitation of liability and damages cap.

9.8 Costs and Fees

Unless otherwise required by applicable law:

  • Each party shall bear its own attorneys’ fees and costs
  • Arbitration filing fees shall be shared equally
  • The arbitrator may award fees and costs to the prevailing party where legally permitted

9.9 Finality

The arbitrator’s decision shall be final, binding, and enforceable in any court of competent jurisdiction, including courts in Monmouth County, New Jersey.

9.10 Survival

This arbitration agreement survives the completion, cancellation, or termination of the Competition and applies to any dispute arising after participation.
